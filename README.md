# Data Professional Survey Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project explores survey data from data professionals to better understand their career experiences, salary ranges, programming language preferences, and job satisfaction.

Using Microsoft Power BI, I developed an interactive dashboard to organize survey responses into visualizations that make it easier to identify patterns and compare different aspects of the data profession.

The project demonstrates my ability to work with structured data, develop data visualizations, and communicate analytical findings through a dashboard.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze the distribution of respondents across different data-related job titles.
- Explore salary ranges across professional roles.
- Identify popular programming languages among data professionals.
- Examine job satisfaction in areas such as salary and work-life balance.
- Explore respondents' experiences entering the data profession.
- Present survey results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel** — Dataset storage and initial data inspection.

## Dataset

The project uses a survey dataset containing 630 records and 28 columns before any project-specific cleaning or transformation.

The dataset includes information about:

- Job titles and professional roles
- Salary ranges
- Industry
- Favorite programming languages
- Job satisfaction
- Difficulty of entering the data profession
- Country of residence
- Age and educational background

**Dataset source:**

https://github.com/AlexTheAnalyst/Power-BI/blob/main/Power%20BI%20-%20Final%20Project.xlsx

## Data Preparation

The dataset was inspected before being used for analysis.

The preparation process includes reviewing column names, checking data types, examining missing values, and identifying fields that are relevant to the analysis.

## Key Findings

The dashboard is designed to explore patterns in salary, career preferences, and job satisfaction among survey respondents.

These findings describe the survey respondents and should not automatically be generalized to all data professionals.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the verified findings, potential recommendations may include:

- **Career planning:** Use salary and job-title comparisons as a starting point when researching career paths in data.
- **Skills development:** Examine programming language preferences to identify skills worth investigating for specific data-related roles.
- **Workplace improvement:** Use job satisfaction results to identify areas that may warrant further investigation.
- **Further analysis:** Compare results across roles, industries, and countries where the sample size supports meaningful comparisons.

The final recommendations should be linked directly to the actual findings rather than assumptions.

## Limitations

- The analysis reflects the available survey responses and may not represent the entire data professional population.
- Salary results are based on the salary information and categories provided by the dataset.
- Survey responses may reflect individual perceptions and preferences.
- Findings should be interpreted in the context of the dataset's sample size, collection method, and date.
- Data preparation and source limitations should be considered when interpreting the results.
